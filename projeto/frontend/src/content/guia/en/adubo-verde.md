## In one sentence

Green manure is a plant grown to **leave nutrients in the soil**, not to harvest.

## Why it matters

Legumes, like crotalaria, fix nitrogen from the air. According to Embrapa, this reduces the need for nitrogen fertilizer.

## In your field

Among the app's options, crotalaria is the legume. It also controls soil nematodes and covers the soil well.

How much nitrogen is left in the soil changes with the soil, the cultivar and the rain. That figure is up to your agronomist.
