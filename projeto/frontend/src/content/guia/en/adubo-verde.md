## In one sentence

Green manure is a plant grown to **leave nutrients in the soil**, not to harvest.

## Why it matters

Legumes, like crotalaria, fix nitrogen from the air with the help of bacteria on their roots. The next crop uses it, and you can use less nitrogen fertilizer.

## In your field

In the app, only crotalaria leaves nitrogen: **80 kg/ha**, and a score of **30 of 30** on the nitrogen item. The other options get 0.

The app uses the average value as a team hypothesis. The real number changes with the soil, the cultivar and the rain.

See the Soil row in [Compare](/opcoes). Get to know [crotalaria](/guia/crotalaria) and [straw](/guia/palhada), which it also leaves.

Green manure only works if the plant grows well. That is why planting in time and the rain of the first 30 days count. See [the planting deadline](/guia/prazo-de-plantio-zarc).
