## In one sentence

Green manure is a plant grown to **leave nutrients in the soil**, not to harvest.

## Why it matters

Legumes such as crotalaria make use of biological fixation of nitrogen from the air. An article in the journal Ciência e Agrotecnologia points to this use as one of the most viable options to reduce production costs and the risk of pollution.

## In your field

Among the app's options, crotalaria is the legume. In a study published in Revista Caatinga, it was also an option against root lesion nematodes.

How much nitrogen is left in the soil changes with the soil, the cultivar and the rain. That figure is up to your agronomist.
