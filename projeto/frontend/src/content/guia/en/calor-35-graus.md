## In one sentence

The app looks at **heat at flowering**, using each day's maximum temperature measured by NASA POWER.

## Why it matters

NASA POWER is a NASA project focused on agroclimatology, among others, and provides daily climate data since 1981. The app takes each day's maximum temperature from it.

## In your field

The app counts very hot days in the crop's most delicate phase, flowering, and uses that to say whether the year "worked". See [What "it worked" means](/guia/deu-certo).

Heat shows up as a block on the [What the satellite saw](/satelite) panel.

Sowing within the ZARC deadline helps control when flowering falls. See [the planting deadline](/guia/prazo-de-plantio-zarc).

How much heat your crop tolerates at flowering is up to your agronomist.
