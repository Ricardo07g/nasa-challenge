## In one sentence

A nematode is a very tiny soil critter that attacks the roots and takes strength from the plant.

## Why it matters

Some nematodes spend the off-season as eggs in root remains. That is why the next crop is the most affected. Planting the same plant again feeds the problem.

## In your field

The way to reduce it is to rotate with a species the nematode cannot use, like crotalaria. You can tell the app whether there are nematodes in the area.

If you say yes, the app changes the math:

- The pests column counts 1.5 times more in the soil score.
- The [3-year plan](/plano) only shows plans with crotalaria or millet, when one of them fits the window.

See [crotalaria](/guia/crotalaria) and [pearl millet](/guia/milheto), the two options the app uses against nematodes.

Which nematode species you have and which resistant variety to use are up to your agronomist.
