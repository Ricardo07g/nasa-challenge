## In one sentence

The app repeats the water math with the climate that models project for **2041 to 2060**.

## Why it matters

What worked in the past may not work in the future. Seeing both side by side helps choose a rotation for a changing climate.

## In your field

The data come from NASA's **NEX-GDDP-CMIP6**: projections from 35 CMIP6 climate models, bias-corrected and refined to a 0.25° grid (about 25 km), day by day.

The app uses the difference between the future and past climate and applies it to the climate of your spot. It shows "would work in so many of 20 years", from a minimum to a maximum, depending on the model.

This is a **projection, not a forecast**, and it does not apply to any single year.
