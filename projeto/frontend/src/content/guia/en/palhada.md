## In one sentence

Straw is the **layer of plant residue** that covers the soil after harvest.

## Why it matters

Straw on the soil is the most important factor in absorbing the impact of raindrops and keeps soil grains from breaking loose. In no-till, it also helps reduce weeds and conserve soil moisture.

## In your field

In a study in Goiás, pearl millet made more straw at the start of the off-season, and brachiaria grasses built up straw and nutrients until its end. Leaving the soil fallow forms no new straw.
