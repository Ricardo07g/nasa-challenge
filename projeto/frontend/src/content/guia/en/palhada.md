## In one sentence

Straw is the **layer of plant residue** that covers the soil after harvest.

## Why it matters

According to Embrapa, straw protects the soil from the impact of raindrops and from heat, and helps retain moisture. Thick straw also prevents erosion and controls weeds. As it decomposes, it gives back part of the nutrients.

## In your field

Pearl millet gives a lot of straw for no-till, and brachiaria grass, in the Santa Fé System, forms thick straw. Leaving the soil fallow forms no new straw.

See what each option leaves in the Soil row of [Compare](/opcoes) and in the [Suggestion](/resposta). If you want straw, look at [pearl millet](/guia/milheto) or [corn with brachiaria](/guia/milho-braquiaria).

How much straw your soil needs is up to your agronomist.
