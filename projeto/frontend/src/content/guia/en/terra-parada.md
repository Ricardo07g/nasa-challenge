## In one sentence

Harvest the soybeans and **plant nothing** until the next soybeans. In the app, "Leave it fallow".

## Why it matters

With no plant, no new straw forms. Erosion studies show that straw on the soil is the most important factor in absorbing the impact of raindrops, which keeps soil grains from breaking loose. That is why fallow is the baseline to compare the other options.

## Get to know it

- **Use:** nothing. It is the choice of not planting.
- **Straw:** forms no new straw.
- **Soil:** more exposed to the impact of rain.
- **In the app:** it is the comparison for the other options.

## Step by step

1. **Harvest the soybeans** and leave the area unplanted.
2. **Compare** with the other options in the [Choose](/resposta) tab.
3. **Talk to your agronomist** about managing the fallow area.

## In your field

The app shows the history of fallow years in [Your land's X-ray](/satelite) and compares fallow with the other options in the [Choose](/resposta) tab. See what changes when the soil is covered in [straw](/guia/palhada).
