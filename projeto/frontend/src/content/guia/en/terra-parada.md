## In one sentence

Harvest the soybeans and **plant nothing** until the next soybeans. In the app, "Leave it fallow".

## Why it matters

With no plant, there is no new straw. Embrapa notes that straw protects the soil from the impact of rain, and erosion comes back when straw is short. That is why fallow is the baseline to compare the other options.

## Get to know it

- **Use:** nothing. It is the choice of not planting.
- **Straw:** forms no new straw.
- **In the app:** it is the comparison for the other options.

## Step by step

1. **Harvest the soybeans** and leave the area unplanted.
2. **Compare** with the other options in [Compare](/opcoes).
3. **Talk to your agronomist** about managing the fallow area.

## In your field

The app shows the history of fallow years in [What the satellite saw](/satelite) and compares fallow with the other options in [Compare](/opcoes). See what changes when the soil is covered in [straw](/guia/palhada).
