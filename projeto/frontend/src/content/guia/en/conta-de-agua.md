## In one sentence

The app treats the soil as a **bucket**: rain fills it, the plant empties it, and what is missing is thirst.

## Why it matters

What decides the second crop is not just the year's rain. It is how much water the soil holds and how much the plant uses each day.

## In your field

Every day, the app does the math:

1. **Rain comes in**, seen by the GPM IMERG satellite.
2. **The plant uses water.** The use comes from heat and sun (NASA POWER) and from the crop.
3. **The bucket size** comes from the soil type: clay, silt and sand, from the SoilGrids map.
4. **The starting point** can be the moisture measured by the SMAP satellite, when there is any.

When the bucket runs dry, water is short. How much was missing enters the article [What "it worked" means](/guia/deu-certo).

To make use of the rain that already falls, keep the soil covered: cover helps hold moisture. See [straw](/guia/palhada).

In the [3-year plan](/plano), "rain used" shows this for each year. The [Suggestion](/resposta) has the Water row.
