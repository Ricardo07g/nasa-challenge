## In one sentence

El Niño and La Niña are phases when the **Pacific Ocean** is warmer or colder than normal.

## Why it matters

The app only reports each year's phase. It does not enter the score math and is not treated as a drought forecast.

## In your field

The phase is measured by NOAA, in the United States, not by NASA. The measure is the ONI: the sea temperature anomaly in the Niño 3.4 region, as a 3-month average. NOAA uses:

- **El Niño:** **+0.5 °C** or more.
- **La Niña:** **-0.5 °C** or less.

For a phase to count, it must last at least 5 seasons in a row.

The app puts a mark on each second-crop year, in the [Suggestion](/resposta), and shows NOAA's forecast as an autumn signal in the [3-year plan](/plano).
