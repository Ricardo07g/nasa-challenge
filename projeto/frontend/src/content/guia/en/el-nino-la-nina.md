## In one sentence

El Niño and La Niña are phases when the Pacific Ocean is warmer or colder than normal.

## Why it matters

These phases can change the rain in many regions. The app only reports each year's phase. It does not enter the score math.

## In your field

The phase is measured by NOAA, in the United States, not by NASA. The measure is the ONI: sea temperature in a region of the Pacific, as a 3-month average. The app uses +0.5 °C or more for El Niño and -0.5 °C or less for La Niña.

For each second crop, the app averages the ONI from December to April (DJF, JFM and FMA) and puts a mark on the year, in the [Suggestion](/resposta).

The autumn signal uses NOAA's forecast and looks at how the first-year option did in years of the same phase. That is a team hypothesis. See the signal in the [3-year plan](/plano).

There is no simple "El Niño equals drought" rule. That is why the mark is only a heads-up.
