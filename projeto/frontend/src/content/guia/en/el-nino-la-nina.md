## In one sentence

El Niño and La Niña are phases when the **Pacific Ocean** is warmer or colder than normal.

## Why it matters

The app only reports each year's phase. It does not enter the score math and is not treated as a drought forecast.

In Southeast Brazil, a study of 11 La Niñas found that the rains tend to start later in the Triângulo Mineiro and that summer gets drier in much of Minas Gerais. The end of the rainy season did not change consistently.

## In your field

The phase is measured by NOAA, in the United States, not by NASA. Since February 2026, the official measure is the **RONI**: the sea temperature anomaly in the Niño 3.4 region, as a 3-month average, minus the tropical average. NOAA uses:

- **El Niño:** **+0.5 °C** or more.
- **La Niña:** **-0.5 °C** or less.

For a phase to count, it must last at least 5 seasons in a row.

The app puts a mark on each second-crop year, in the [Choose](/resposta) tab, and shows NOAA's forecast as an autumn signal in the [3-year plan](/plano).
