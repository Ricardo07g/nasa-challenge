## In one sentence

A cover legume that **fixes nitrogen from the air** and helps lower some nematodes.

## Why it matters

The best known are *Crotalaria juncea*, *C. ochroleuca* and *C. spectabilis*. They fix nitrogen from the air and leave it in the straw.

In a field study in Mato Grosso, crotalarias lowered the root lesion nematode before and early in the soybean season. At the soybean harvest, the difference was gone. The best option in the study was *C. spectabilis*.

## Get to know it

- **Use:** cover and nitrogen in the soil.
- **Nematodes:** helps lower the root lesion nematode and, *C. spectabilis*, also root-knot nematodes.
- **Timing:** it is a short-day plant; sown later, it makes less mass.
- **When to plant:** there is no ZARC for crotalaria or other cover crops.

## Step by step

1. **Talk to your agronomist** about timing: there is no ZARC deadline for it.
2. **Sow** right after the soybeans.
3. **Let it grow** and cover the soil.
4. **Plant the soybeans** on the cover.
