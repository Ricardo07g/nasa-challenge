## In one sentence

A grain cereal that handles water shortage better than corn.

## Why it matters

In the off-season the rain fails. Sorghum is an option when drought risk is scary.

## Get to know it

- Use: grain.
- Water: it asks for less than corn (coefficient 1.05 at the peak).
- Leaves in the soil: medium straw (20 of 40 points) and some help against pests (15 of 30).
- Fears: drought and heat of 35 °C or more at flowering, but it takes more than corn.

## Step by step

1. Sow right after the soybean harvest. The app's ZARC limit is March 20.
2. Flowering comes at about 65 days. The whole cycle takes about 115.
3. Watch the rain at flowering.
4. Harvest the grain in June or July. Leave the straw for the next soybeans.
