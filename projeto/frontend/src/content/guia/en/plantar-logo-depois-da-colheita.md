## In one sentence

In the second crop, the time between the soybean harvest and planting the next plant **counts**.

## Why it matters

Each crop has a planting deadline in the ZARC, published by the Ministry of Agriculture. On many farms, the second crop is sown little by little, as the soybean harvest advances, which avoids losing everything at once.

## In your field

On the [When you harvest](/quando) screen, the app shows, for each harvest time, the best option and in how many of the 20 years of satellite rain it worked. The best option can change from one time to another.

Talk to your agronomist about how to gain days between harvest and sowing.

See [The planting deadline and the ZARC](/guia/prazo-de-plantio-zarc) and [When the rain ends](/guia/quando-a-chuva-acaba).
