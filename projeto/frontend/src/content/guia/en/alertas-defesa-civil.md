## In one sentence

Civil Defense sends disaster alerts to your phone, and you can sign up for free.

## Why it matters

Heavy rain, flash floods and landslides arrive fast. The official warning counts for more than any estimate from the app.

## In your field

Raiz Orbital warnings help you prepare, but they **do not replace** the official alerts. They sit at the bottom of [Alerts](/avisos).

To receive the alerts:

1. **SMS:** send the postal code (CEP) of your area to the number 40199. You can register more than one CEP.
2. **WhatsApp:** save the number (61) 2034-4611 and send a "hello".
3. **Defesa Civil Alerta:** on compatible phones, with 4G or 5G, the warning arrives by itself, with no sign-up.

In an emergency, call:

- **199**, Civil Defense: floods, landslides and risk areas.
- **193**, Fire Department: fires, accidents and rescues.

Near a field with fire or flood, call first.
