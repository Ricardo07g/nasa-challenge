## In one sentence

"It worked" is a year when the plant had no water or heat stress in its most delicate phase.

## Why it matters

The app looks at 20 years of climate, from 2005 to 2024, and counts in how many of them the option would have worked. That count becomes the rain label: Good, Fair or Weak.

## In your field

A year is a stress year when one of these happens:

- The water index (from 0 to 1, how much of the water it needed the plant got) falls below 0.55 in the critical phase.
- It is 35 °C or more on 3 days or more in the critical phase.

The grain's critical phase runs from 15 days before to 15 days after flowering. For cover crops, stress is an index below 0.50 in the first 30 days.

The Good label needs 17 or more stress-free years out of 20. Fair runs from 12 to 16. Below that, Weak.

See the number in the [Suggestion](/resposta) and the open math in [How the math works](/como-funciona). Also understand [the water math](/guia/conta-de-agua).
