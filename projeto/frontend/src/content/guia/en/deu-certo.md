## In one sentence

**"It worked"** is a year when the plant had no water or heat stress in its most delicate phase, flowering.

## Why it matters

The app looks at **20 years** of climate, from 2005 to 2024, and counts in how many of them the option would have worked. That count becomes the rain label: Good, Fair or Weak, depending on the number of years.

## In your field

The rain comes from the GPM IMERG satellite and the heat from NASA POWER. For each year, the app checks two things:

- Whether the plant ran short of **water** in the most delicate phase.
- Whether it was **too hot** in that phase.

If either happened, the year does not count as "worked".
