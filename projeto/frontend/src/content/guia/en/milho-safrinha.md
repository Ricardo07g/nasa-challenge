## In one sentence

Corn planted right after soybeans, to harvest grain in winter. In the app it shows as "Corn alone".

## Why it matters

It is the best-known grain option. But if sowing is late, flowering falls at the end of the rains, and water runs short just when the plant needs it most.

## Get to know it

- Use: grain.
- Water: it asks for a lot. At its peak, the app uses a coefficient of 1.20, the highest of all.
- Leaves in the soil: little straw (20 of 40 points) and no nitrogen.
- Fears: drought and heat of 35 °C or more at flowering.

## Step by step

1. Sow right after the soybean harvest. The app's ZARC limit is February 28.
2. Flowering comes at about 60 days. The whole cycle takes about 120.
3. Watch the rain and the heat at flowering.
4. Harvest in winter. If you can, cover the soil before the next soybeans.
