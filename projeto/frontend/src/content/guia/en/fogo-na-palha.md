## In one sentence

The app warns when there was **burned area** near your point, with NASA satellite data.

## Why it matters

The data comes from the MCD64A1 product, from NASA's Terra and Aqua satellites. It maps burned area month by month, in 500 m squares, and says on which day of the year the fire happened.

## In your field

On the [What the satellite saw](/satelite) screen, the fire alert tells in how many of the last years there was burned area near your point. A small fire may not show up.

Straw protects the soil, and bare soil loses that protection. See [Straw: why cover the soil](/guia/palhada).

How to protect your field from fire is up to your agronomist.

In case of fire, call **193** (Fire Department). See also [Official Civil Defense alerts](/guia/alertas-defesa-civil).
