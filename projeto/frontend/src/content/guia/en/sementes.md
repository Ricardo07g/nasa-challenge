## In one sentence

Good, legal seed is where the crop starts: check who you buy from and what is written on the package.

## Why it matters

The second crop has little time: every day after the soybean harvest counts. Seed that emerges poorly leaves gaps in the field, and replanting costs days the second crop does not have.

Brazil's seed law (Law 10,711, from 2003) sets some rules that help the farmer:

- **Who to buy from:** whoever produces or sells seed must be registered in the National Registry of Seeds and Seedlings (Renasem).
- **Package:** all seed must be identified on the package, label or tag.
- **Certified seed:** it went through a certification process, with control of production.
- **Documents:** seed that is sold comes with documents. Keep the invoice and those papers.

## In your field

Before paying, read the label and ask the seller about the germination and vigor of the lot. Also ask whether the seed is treated and with what.

The amount of seed per hectare, the variety and the treatment are up to your agronomist. The app does not recommend a variety or a rate.
