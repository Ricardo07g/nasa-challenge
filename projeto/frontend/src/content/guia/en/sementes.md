## In one sentence

Good, legal seed is the start of the crop: check **who you buy from** and what the package says.

## Why it matters

The second crop has little time: every day after the soybean harvest counts. Seed that emerges poorly leaves gaps in the field, and replanting costs days the second crop does not have.

Brazil's seed law (Law 10,711, of 2003) gives some rules that help the grower:

- **Who to buy from:** whoever produces or sells seed must be registered in the National Seed and Seedling Registry (Renasem).
- **Packaging:** all seed must be identified on the package, label or tag.
- **Certified seed:** it went through a certification process, with production control.
- **Documents:** the seed sold comes with documents. Keep the invoice and these papers.

## In your field

Before you pay:

- **Read the label.**
- **Ask** the seller for the lot's germination and vigor.
- **Ask** whether the seed is treated, and with what.

The amount of seed per hectare, the variety and the treatment are up to your agronomist. The app does not recommend a variety or a rate.
