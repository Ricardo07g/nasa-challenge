## In one sentence

**Corn and brachiaria grass** sown together: the corn gives grain and the grass covers the soil.

## Why it matters

This intercrop became known as the **Santa Fé System**. In no-till, brachiaria sown with corn serves as pasture from late summer to early spring and then forms the straw for the next crop. In a Cerrado study, brachiaria grasses built up straw and nutrients until the end of the off-season.

## Get to know it

- **Use:** grain and cover.
- **How it works:** corn and brachiaria sown together.
- **When to plant:** the deadline comes from the ZARC for your municipality. The second-crop corn and brachiaria intercrop was added to the ZARC.

## Step by step

1. **Check the deadline** in the ZARC for your municipality.
2. **Sow the grass together with the corn.**
3. **Harvest the corn.** The grass stays and covers the soil.
4. **Plant the next soybeans** on the straw, in no-till.
